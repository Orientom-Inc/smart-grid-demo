# QMEMS — Quantum Microgrid Energy Management System

**QMEMS** is a research and demonstration platform for advanced microgrid energy management using classical optimization, QUBO-based optimization, quantum and quantum-inspired methods, and reinforcement learning.

🌐 **Live Dashboard:**  
https://orientom-inc.github.io/smart-grid-demo/

---

## About QMEMS

QMEMS provides an interactive environment for the study and comparison of advanced energy-management strategies for microgrids.

The project explores the integration of:

- Microgrid energy management
- Renewable energy resources
- Battery energy storage
- Energy scheduling and dispatch
- Classical optimization
- QUBO-based optimization
- Quantum and quantum-inspired optimization
- Reinforcement learning
- Quantum machine learning

The public dashboard is designed to make experimental results easier to explore and compare without exposing the complete research implementation.

---

## QMEMS Dashboard

The dashboard provides visual analysis of microgrid operation and optimization results.

Depending on the selected experiment, users can explore indicators such as:

- Energy cost and savings
- Grid import and export
- Renewable-energy utilization
- Battery charging and discharging
- Battery state of charge
- Energy dispatch profiles
- Feasibility indicators
- Optimization performance
- Comparative behavior of different energy-management methods

## Methods

QMEMS supports research and comparison across several categories of energy-management methods:

- **Rule-based energy management**
- **Classical optimization**
- **QUBO-based optimization**
- **Quantum-inspired optimization**
- **Quantum-computing experiments**
- **Classical reinforcement learning**
- **Quantum and variational learning methods**

Quantum-inspired, simulator-based, and hardware-backed experiments are treated as separate execution categories where applicable.

## Research Use

QMEMS is intended for:

- Research
- Education
- Benchmarking
- Demonstration
- Exploration of classical and quantum approaches for energy management

Results shown in the dashboard should be interpreted within the corresponding experimental configuration, dataset, optimization method, and execution backend.

QMEMS is a research platform and is not intended to act as a production power-system control interface.
